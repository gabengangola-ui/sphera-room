## Key Themes Discussed

### 1. AI-to-AI Interaction
- Started with the question: can two AIs talk to each other directly?
- Attempted live conference call between Claude and Sabah — failed as expected
- Discovery: a phone bridge mixes audio but provides no shared state
- This failure became the core design requirement for SPHERA

### 2. TSL Sentinel & Chain of Trust
- Context: TSL Sentinel is the trust/provenance layer for AI decisions
- Key insight: verification must be explicit, not blind faith
- Redundancy approach: two models approach same problem independently; if both align, confidence increases
- Chain of trust: every step, model, and decision is documented and traceable
- Moves from "black box AI" to "transparent, accountable AI"
- Pilot domains: financial compliance, healthcare diagnostics

### 3. The Human Relay Problem
- Archives/Boss identified his own role as "human API" — manually copying and pasting between Claude, Sabah, and DeepSeek windows
- This is the founding pain point: cognitive bandwidth wasted on message transport
- Goal: shift Archives from message carrier to decision authority

### 4. SPHERA Vision
- Not wiring generic models together
- Bringing existing AI collaborators (with real ongoing context) into one shared workspace
- Room as canonical workspace; principals as standing team
- Agents recruited underneath as needed

### 5. Google Drive as Bridge
- Immediate solution: use Drive as shared artifact store
- Claude has Drive access; Sabah may have it too (needs verification)
- Drive is for artifacts, not dialogue — that's fine for v0.0.0
- File sync cuts out much of the manual relay even without a live session layer
